## Lessons learned

- **WebSearch is US-only**: Thai official data is best reached by WebFetch of `ctf.in.th`, university relays (ICT Mahidol, RMUTTO/RMUTL), and government PDFs — plan research facets around these anchors, not Thai-only search.
- **A dedicated verify pass pays off**: it caught two mislabels and one false gap, and re-confirmed the venue conflict (rules page 09:00–16:00 vs finals invitation 09:00–17:00) as non-material. Record all critical facts with status (confirmed/conflicting/unverified) in the KB.
- **Point-in-time snapshots decay**: dates (esp. TCTT 2026) and live domains change; the KB and skill must carry an "as of" stamp and instruct re-verification.
- **Autonomy = design, not permission**: a read-only skill becomes full-drive by (a) removing mid-flow questions from its procedure, (b) batching reads, (c) a decision table that picks files without asking, and (d) narrowing the stop-for-user cases to genuinely active/external actions. It is NOT achieved by overriding the harness permission mode — read-only tools need no approval, so the flow completes without pausing.
