# TCTT 2025 — Overview

## What it is

**Thailand Cyber Top Talent (TCTT / Thailand Cyber Top Talents)** is Thailand's national cybersecurity Capture-The-Flag competition, run annually by the National Cyber Security Agency (NCSA / สกมช.) with Huawei Technologies (Thailand) as long-time co-organizer/sponsor. The 2025 edition is **THCTT 2025**. It is a workforce-development mechanism, not just a skills contest: the official page states it "มิได้เป็นเพียงเวทีเพื่อวัดระดับความสามารถเท่านั้น แต่ยังเป็นกลไกสำคัญในการสร้างและพัฒนากำลังคนที่มีศักยภาพในสาขาความมั่นคงปลอดภัยไซเบอร์" (is not only a stage to measure ability but a key mechanism to build and develop a capable cyber-security workforce). [high]

## Legal basis

Operates under Thailand's **Cybersecurity Act B.E. 2562 (2019)**. The NCSA (National Cyber Security Committee Office) runs TCTT alongside its other flagship cyber activities: the ASEAN Cyber Shield (ACS) hacking contest, and Women Thailand Cyber Top Talent. [high]

## Organizers & platform

- **National Cyber Security Agency (NCSA / สกมช.)** — organizer. [high]
- **Huawei Technologies (Thailand) Co., Ltd.** — co-organizer/sponsor (official wording: "จัดขึ้นโดยความร่วมมือระหว่าง... สกมช. และบริษัท หัวเว่ย เทคโนโลยี (ประเทศไทย) จำกัด"). [high]
- **Thailand National Cyber Academy (THNCA)** — operates the platform/announcement channels. [high]
- **CTF Thailand (ctf.in.th)** — hosts the official 2025 page and the competition platform ("Powered by CTF THAILAND | ctf.in.th"). [high]

## The three divisions (levels)

| Division | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Junior** (มัธยมศึกษา) | secondary-school students at a school/institution in Thailand | student ID card required at registration |
| **Senior** (อุดมศึกษา) | higher-education students at a Thai university/institution, **age ≤ 30 on registration date** | student ID card required |
| **Open** (ประชาชนทั่วไป) | CII agencies, government, private sector, and the **general Thai public** (Thai nationality required) | no separate government track — govt staff compete here |

[high]

## Scale (2025)

- **1,352 teams** registered (Junior 518 / Senior 496 / Open 338) per the official CTF Thailand statistics page. **These are teams, not individuals.** The finals invitation describes selecting from "more than 3,000 contestants nationwide" (~1,352 teams × up to 3 members). [high]
- Senior figure (496) independently corroborated by ICT Mahidol University ("top 10 of 496"). [high]
