## Edition details

### 2021 (first edition)
- "Thailand Cyber Top Talents 2021" — described as the first cyber-defense competition of its kind in Thailand; brought together "over 800 outstanding IT students and personnel". NCSA + Huawei. [medium]

### 2022
- 3 tiers (secondary school / higher education / general public); teams of 3, students from same region. Registration until 24 Sep 2022. Round 1 online Jeopardy CTF 1 Oct 2022; final "Attack the Virtual World" 22 Oct 2022. [high]
- Awarded **9 winners** across tiers: 128,000 (school), 150,000 (university), 180,000 (general) = **521,000 THB total**, plus entry to the regional **Cyber SEA Game**. Award ceremony (Nov 2022) presided over by the DES Permanent Secretary; Huawei Thailand CEO present. [high]
- Context: NCSA + Huawei MoU (5 Aug 2022) to train 4,000 Thai IT personnel over 3 years via Huawei's e-Lab platform. [medium]

### 2023
- Qualifier 16 Sep 2023 drew **2,323 participants / 831 teams** (online Jeopardy); **30 teams** advanced to the onsite final 30 Sep 2023 under the "Attack the Virtual World" theme, presided over by DES Minister Prasert Jantararuangthong. [high]
- Overall winner: team **Rebooster** (Royal Police Cadet Academy Cyber Club) — their reported "second time" winning TCTT. Members: Pol Cdt Sudrit Vongsuwan, Pol Cdt Tassanai Manit, Pol Cdt Wannakorn Nunpradit. Also competed at Cyber SEA Game. [high] (Bangkok Post paywalled — details via summary) [medium]
- Prize pool > 521,000 THB + IT equipment + certificates; winners represent Thailand at Cyber SEA Game. [high]

### 2024
- Retained 3-tier structure (renamed **Junior / Senior / Open**), teams ≤3, same-region rule. Registration until 25 Sep 2024 via `thcybertoptalent.in.th`. Qualifier online Jeopardy 28 Sep 2024; onsite final "Attack the Virtual World" 12 Oct 2024. [high]
- Partner/supporting organizations: SIAM, THAWAY, HACK, GOVERNTRUST, TB-CERT, CTFAD, THNCAA. [medium]
- TCTT 2024 + Women TCTT 2024 award ceremony held **13 Jan 2025**, presented by Assistant Minister to the PM's Office Ms. Nahathai Thiuphaingam on behalf of NCSA. [medium]

### Women Thailand Cyber Top Talent (parallel track, from 2023)
- **2023**: levels WJunior/WSenior/WOpen; winners included NR01 (Junior), hacKEr4nDtHECA7-1 (Open); national first runner-up **Need 2 Know** (Thammasat University). [medium]
- **2024**: prize pool **333,000 THB**; online qualifier 12 Jan 2025, onsite final 23 Jan 2025 at Sirikit National Convention Center during CYBERSEC ASIA × Thailand International Cyber Week 2025. First prizes: 30k (WJunior) / 40k (WSenior) / 50k (WOpen). 30 finalist teams. Winner: **CVE2025Moodeng** (KBTG); runner-up SamoonNaKarunn (KMUTT). [medium]

## Recurring themes

- **Stable structure**: categories, division names, and the >521,000 THB prize pool have been remarkably stable year-over-year since 2022. [medium]
- **Official purpose**: produce enough Thai cybersecurity personnel, raise public threat awareness, give students/staff/public a chance to develop cyber skills; winners represent Thailand at regional/international events (notably Cyber SEA Game). [high]
