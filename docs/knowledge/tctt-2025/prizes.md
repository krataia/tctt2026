# TCTT 2025 — Prizes, Opportunities & Community

## Cash prize pool — over 521,000 THB

Official page ("เงินรางวัลรวมกว่า 521,000 บาท") — verified and internally consistent (per-level totals sum to 521k when including the 3,000 THB consolation for each non-podium finalist team). [high]

| Level | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | Consolation (per finalist team) | Level total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Open** (201,000) | 100,000 | 50,000 | 30,000 | 3,000 | 201,000 |
| **Senior** (171,000) | 90,000 | 40,000 | 20,000 | 3,000 | 171,000 |
| **Junior** (149,000) | 80,000 | 30,000 | 18,000 | 3,000 | 149,000 |

- Every finalist team in all levels received a 3,000 THB consolation prize. [high]
- Participants passing the selection criteria receive an official **certificate (เกียรติบัตร) and prizes from NCSA** — per the NCSA letter, which does not itself publish cash amounts. [high]
- The finals invitation separately mentioned ~130,000 THB of sponsor support prizes (gift vouchers), complementary to the cash pool. [high]

## International opportunity — Cyber SEA Game 2025

- The **champion team represents Thailand** at the regional **Cyber SEA Game 2025** ("จะได้รับโอกาสเป็นตัวแทนประเทศไทยเข้าร่วมการแข่งขัน Cyber SEA Game 2025"). [high]
- Cyber SEA Game 2025 was held in **Bangkok 16–17 Oct 2025**: **champion Vietnam, runner-up Singapore, 3rd Indonesia**; seven standout individuals selected for Team ASEAN at ICC 2025. [high]
- **Unverified:** the TCTT 2025 champion team is reported (by its own member's blog) as **"Don't Know Everything"** (Senior 1st). No official result list, no independent news, and the official Cyber SEA Game results page lists no Thailand team — treat the name as a lead only. [unverified]

## Sibling programs (do not confuse with the main TCTT 2025)

- **NCSA x NTBC Cyber Top Talent 2025** — NBTC (กสทช.) + NCSA + Huawei program for schools in **remote/border areas**; **110 teams** on the NCSA MOOC platform via USO Net centers. Winners: Ban Chong Phli School (Krabi) 1st, Ban Pak Phayun School (Phatthalung) 2nd, Wat Nanon School (Trang) 3rd — prize was an all-expense **study trip to Huawei HQ/R&D in China (8–12 May 2025)**, not cash. [high]
- **Women Thailand Cyber Top Talent 2025** — separate all-women edition; its top individual scorer received an MVP medal. Final-round date inconsistent across sources (Jan 2025 vs Jan 2026). [medium]

## Awards ceremony

PM **Anutin Charnvirakul** honored TCTT 2025 winners at the **Thailand Cybersecurity Excellence Awards 2025** ceremony at Government House, alongside winners of the 3rd ASEAN Cyber Shield (ACS) hacking contest and Women TCTT 2025 (NCSA runs these under the Cybersecurity Act B.E. 2562). Exact ceremony date not stated (article dated 22 Apr 2026). [medium]

## Community coverage

- Multiple Thai CTF participants published **detailed TCTT 2025 challenge write-ups** on Medium (corra_nt, pammer434/d3kc@rt_1, pipe2bot, fifafayya, Kiatisak) and blog.noonomyen.com / blog.k1god.com, aggregated by the Thailand CTF community portal **ctf.in.th**. [high]
- Example highlights from write-ups: "First Blood" on the Shadow-Hive (registry `.hiv`) forensic challenge; `not_doom` buffer overflow (offset 40, password `0xdeadb33fdeadb33f`); `hidden_payload` (AES-128-ECB ISO, `J`×16 key); Scratch 3 "Get High Fly Flag"; heavy participant use of AI tools to build payloads. [medium]
