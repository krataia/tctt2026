# TCTT 2025 — Format & Rounds

## Two rounds, no semi-final

Official rules: "รูปแบบการแข่งขัน แบ่งเป็น 2 รอบ" — exactly **two rounds**: an online qualification round and a national final. There is no semi-final. [high]

### Round 1 — Online qualification (รอบคัดเลือก)

- **Online Jeopardy-style CTF**, Sat **30 Aug 2025**, **09:00–16:00 (7 hours)**. [high]
- Teams may solve any challenge in any order; **points vary by difficulty** (higher difficulty = more points). [high]
- Official category list: **Web Application, Digital Forensic, Reverse Engineering & Pwnable, Network Security, Mobile Security, Programming, Cryptography**. [high]
  - Participant write-ups also document **Miscellaneous** and **Network Forensics**, for a reported total of 9 categories in the Online-Senior qualifier. See `categories.md`. [medium]

### Round 2 — National final (รอบชิงชนะเลิศ) — "Attack the Virtual World"

- **Onsite** CTF, Sat **13 Sep 2025**, The **Berkeley Hotel Pratunam, Bangkok (Mayfair Ballroom)**. The official finals invitation explicitly labels it "รอบชิงชนะเลิศ (Onsite)" with a 09:00–17:00 agenda (talks; awards 16:00–17:00); the rules page lists 09:00–16:00 — a minor, non-material discrepancy. [high]
- Format: **Jeopardy-style attack scenarios** — teams extract answers/flags from **servers, user machines, and network devices inside a simulated environment**, both by attacking from outside and by analyzing forensic traces on compromised servers. This is **not** competitive attack-defense. [high]
- Livestreamed on Facebook pages "NSCA Thailand" and "Thailand National Cyber Academy". [high]
- Community write-ups describe two final-round tracks — **Red (offensive)** and **Blue (defensive)**, 20 challenges each. See `categories.md`. [medium]

## Selection to the final

- Qualifier criteria: **highest score + fastest correct submission** ("คัดเลือกจากทีมที่ทำคะแนนได้สูงสุดและส่งคำตอบเร็วที่สุด"). [high]
- **30 teams advance** (10 per level). [high]
- Regional quotas for Junior and Senior, based on school/university location: **Bangkok 2, Central & East (excl. Bangkok) 2, North 2, Northeast 2, South 2**. **Open has no regional restriction.** [high]
- Qualifier results announced **Tue 2 Sep 2025** (posted at ctf.in.th/news/4019/, results embedded as images). [high]

## Scoring

- Round 1: per-challenge point values scaled by difficulty (observed values in write-ups: 100/200/300 etc.). No official per-challenge point table was published. [medium]
- Tie-break: fastest correct submission. [high]

## Format stability

The 2025 format was essentially unchanged from **TCTT 2024** (same 2 rounds, same 3 divisions, teams ≤3) — differing mainly in timing (2025 ran Aug/Sep; 2024 ran Sep/Oct) and scale (1,352 teams in 2025 vs 793 in 2024). See `past-years.md`. [medium]

## Known gaps

- Challenge language (English vs Thai) not officially specified. [gap]
- Full list of the 30 finalist teams and per-division rankings was not retrievable (results were posted as images; scoreboard `cloud.ctf.in.th/score` was empty). [gap]
- Whether the onsite final was additionally remote-monitored is ambiguous (rules page requires webcam+mic for "every round"; the finals invitation says onsite). [gap]
